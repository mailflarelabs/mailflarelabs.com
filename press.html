In one sentence

MailFlare is a macOS menu bar app that puts an unmissable alert on screen when an important Gmail message arrives.

In one paragraph

MailFlare watches Gmail for the few messages you can't afford to miss, from the senders, domains, or subject words you choose, and puts them on screen as a real app window that Focus modes and Do Not Disturb can't hide. By default the alert is a card on the display you're using; it can also be a banner across the top of every display, or a full-screen alert on every display. MailFlare asks Google only for read-only access, does all its work on the Mac, and has no servers. It is made by MailFlare Labs and is coming soon to the Mac App Store.

Facts

  • Name: MailFlare (one word, capital F). Made by MailFlare Labs.
  • Platform: macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later, with a Gmail or Google Workspace account.
  • Availability: coming soon to the Mac App Store.
  • Website: mailflarelabs.com
