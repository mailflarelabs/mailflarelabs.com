Common questions

The alert didn't appear for a message.
Check that a rule matches the sender or subject (rules are case-insensitive; domains also match subdomains), and that alerts aren't paused and quiet hours aren't active. On its first launch MailFlare stays quiet about mail already in your inbox. After that, mail that arrived while it wasn't running alerts when it starts, if the mail is less than 24 hours old.
What does "Sender not verified" mean?
Gmail checks whether mail really comes from the domain in its From address. When that check fails, the alert says the sender is not verified and the address may be forged. Open the message in Gmail before acting on it. To stop such mail from alerting at all, turn on "Only alert if the sender is verified" for the rule. Leave it off for rules about your own company: mail inside the same Google Workspace can't be verified.
Can it alert on every email?
No — a rule with no senders, domains, or keywords never matches. MailFlare is meant for the few messages you can't miss.
Does it read my email?
It reads the sender, subject, and preview of new unread messages on your Mac to check your rules. While setup or Settings is open, it also looks at who's in your recent mail and who you've emailed, to suggest people for your rules; that stays on your Mac and is never saved. It never reads full messages or attachments. See the Privacy Policy.
How do I disconnect my Google account?
Settings → Gmail Account → Sign Out. MailFlare revokes its access with Google and removes the token from your Keychain.
